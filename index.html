What this app is

Phikat Phisadan Studio is a private desktop tool used by the owner of the “พิกัดพิสดาร” (Phikat Phisadan) channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and does one job: it uploads the channel's own short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account, with their titles, descriptions, captions and cover pictures.

  • YouTube: uploads the video, sets its title, description, subtitles and thumbnail, schedules its publish time and posts the channel's own first comment. The app uses YouTube API Services.
  • Facebook: publishes the video as a Reel on the owner's own Page.
  • TikTok: sends the video to the owner's own TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner then posts in the TikTok app.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in, and only to the owner's own accounts.
